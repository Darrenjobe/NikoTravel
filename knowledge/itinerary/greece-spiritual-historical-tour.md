# Destination Profiles

## 1. Athens (Days 1-3 & 24-25)
**Theme:** The Classical & Orthodox Foundation

### Key Sites & Activities
* **The Areopagus (Mars Hill):** Biblical site where Paul preached to the Athenians about the "Unknown God" (Acts 17). Bronze plaque features the Greek text of his speech.
* **Holy Metropolitan Church:** Main cathedral of Athens, housing the sacred relics of Saint Philothei and Patriarch Gregory V.
* **The Parthenon & Acropolis:** Ultimate foundation of classical Greek civilization and UNESCO World Heritage site.
* **Byzantine and Christian Museum:** Houses one of the world's finest collections of early Christian art, icons, and textiles.
* **Holy Apostles of Solakis:** Beautifully preserved 10th-century Byzantine cross-in-square church in the Ancient Agora.
* **Church of Panagia Kapnikarea:** One of the oldest surviving churches in Athens, built around 1050 AD directly over an ancient pagan temple.
* **Monastery of Kaisariani:** Serene 11th-century working monastery on the slopes of Mount Hymettus.
* **Church of St. Demetrios Loumbardiaris:** Quaint 12th-century stone chapel at the base of Philopappou Hill.
* **Ancient Agora & Stoa of Attalos:** Ancient marketplace where Paul debated daily.
* **Temple of Olympian Zeus:** Colossal ancient ruins near the city center.

## 2. Serifos (Days 4-6)
**Theme:** Cycladic Retreat

### Key Sites & Activities
* **Holy Monastery of the Taxiarches:** 16th-century, fortress-like monastery dedicated to Archangels Michael and Gabriel.
* **Church of Agios Konstantinos:** Highest point in Chora, offering stunning views of the Aegean.
* **The Historic Chora Path:** Old stone trail connecting the lower port (Livadi) to the upper village.
* **Church of Panagia (Livadi):** Historic, peaceful church near the main harbor.
* **Psili Ammos Beach:** Widely considered one of the best sandy beaches in the Cyclades.
* **Panagia Skopiani Chapel:** Beautiful, isolated white chapel against the blue sea.
* **Old Miners' Trail (Megalo Livadi):** Remnants of the island's 19th-century iron mines.
* **Castle of Grias:** Rugged ancient ruins of a Hellenistic tower.
* **Archaeological Museum of Serifos:** Small collection of local antiquities.
* **Church of Agios Athanasios:** Central, beautifully frescoed Orthodox church in the main square of Chora.

## 3. Ancient Corinth & Sparta (Days 7-8)
**Theme:** Into the Peloponnese

### Key Sites & Activities
* **The Bema of St. Paul (Corinth):** Exact public judgment seat where Paul stood trial before Gallio (Acts 18).
* **Ancient Corinth Archaeological Site:** Sprawling ruins where Paul lived and worked for 18 months.
* **Acrocorinth & its Chapels (Corinth):** Imposing fortress above the city containing ancient gates and ruined Byzantine chapels.
* **Corinth Canal (Diolkos):** Ancient paved trackway where ships were dragged overland.
* **Mystras (Sparta):** Spectacular, UNESCO-listed ruined Byzantine city on a hillside.
* **The Pantanassa Monastery (Mystras):** The only still-functioning monastery in the ruins of Mystras.
* **Sanctuary of Artemis Orthia (Sparta):** Ancient pagan temple ruins along the Eurotas River.
* **Archaeological Museum of Sparta:** Features ancient artifacts including early Christian finds.
* **Tomb of Leonidas (Sparta):** Traditional resting place of the legendary Spartan king.
* **Museum of the Olive and Greek Olive Oil (Sparta):** Cultural deep-dive into Greece's most sacred crop.

## 4. Patras & Mega Spilaion (Days 9-10)
**Theme:** Saints & Relics

### Key Sites & Activities
* **Cathedral of Saint Andrew (Patras):** Houses the Holy Skull and remains of the X-shaped cross of the Apostle Andrew.
* **Old Church of Saint Andrew (Patras):** Next to the cathedral, featuring beautiful traditional iconography.
* **Monastery of Mega Spilaion:** Built into a massive cave face, home to a miraculous icon of Mary sculpted by Saint Luke.
* **Holy Monastery of Agia Lavra:** Located in Kalavryta, carrying deep roots in Greek Orthodox tradition.
* **Patras Castle:** 6th-century fortress built by Justinian.
* **Roman Odeon of Patras:** Beautifully restored ancient theater.
* **Vlaterei Church (Patras):** Small, deeply historical Byzantine church in the upper town.
* **Holy Monastery of Panagia Makellaria:** Ancient, secluded working monastery carved into the mountainside.
* **Archaeological Museum of Patras:** Features impressive Roman floor mosaics and early Christian artifacts.
* **Odontotos Rack Railway:** Historic train ride from Diakopto through the gorge.

## 5. Delphi (Day 11)
**Theme:** The Ancient Sanctuary

### Key Sites & Activities
* **Hosios Loukas Monastery (Nearby):** Masterpiece of Middle Byzantine architecture and a UNESCO World Heritage site.
* **Temple of Apollo:** Ancient heart of the sanctuary where the Oracle prophesied.
* **The Athenian Treasury:** Reconstructed monument for ancient devotional offerings.
* **The Castalian Spring:** Sacred ancient spring for pilgrim purification.
* **Delphi Archaeological Museum:** Contains phenomenal ancient artifacts, notably the Charioteer of Delphi.
* **The Tholos of Athena Pronaia:** Iconic, circular ancient structure.
* **The Ancient Theatre of Delphi:** Monumental theater offering sweeping views.
* **The Ancient Stadium:** One of the best-preserved ancient stadiums in Greece.
* **Church of Agios Nikolaos:** Beautiful, peaceful modern Orthodox church in Delphi.
* **The Corycian Cave:** Mythological cave high above Delphi used for worship since the Neolithic era.

## 6. Meteora (Days 12-13)
**Theme:** The Monastic Marvels

### Key Sites & Activities
* **Holy Monastery of Great Meteoron:** Largest and oldest active monastery, with a renowned museum.
* **Holy Monastery of Varlaam:** Second-largest monastery, famously displaying the 16th-century net-and-pulley system.
* **Holy Monastery of Rousanou:** Stunning three-story nunnery dedicated to the Transfiguration.
* **Holy Monastery of St. Nicholas Anapausas:** Features exquisite 16th-century frescoes by Theophanes.
* **Holy Monastery of St. Stephen:** Easily accessible nunnery holding the holy skull of Saint Charalambos.
* **Holy Monastery of Holy Trinity:** Most dramatic and difficult-to-reach monastery.
* **Byzantine Church of the Assumption of Virgin Mary (Kalabaka):** 11th-century church at the base of the rocks.
* **Hermit Caves of Badovas:** Ancient, hollowed-out cliffside caves.
* **Meteora Natural History Museum:** Explains the geological formation of the pillars.
* **Psaropetra Panoramic Viewpoint:** Best spot to view monasteries and watch the sunset.

## 7. Thessaloniki (Days 14-16)
**Theme:** Byzantine & Apostolic Hub

### Key Sites & Activities
* **Vlatadon Monastery:** 14th-century site built on the traditional spot where Paul stayed and preached.
* **Church of Saint Demetrius:** Explore the underground crypt where the patron saint was imprisoned and martyred.
* **The Rotunda:** Emperor Galerius' mausoleum turned early Christian church with 4th-century mosaics.
* **Church of Hagia Sophia:** Features breathtaking early dome mosaics.
* **Church of Panagia Chalkeon:** 11th-century Byzantine cross-in-square church.
* **Church of St. Nicholas Orphanos:** Famous for incredibly preserved 14th-century frescoes.
* **Osios David (Latomou Monastery):** Features a highly rare, beardless Christ mosaic.
* **The White Tower:** Iconic seaside fortress.
* **Byzantine Bath of the Upper Town:** Only surviving public bath in Greece from the Byzantine period.
* **Archaeological Museum of Thessaloniki:** Extensive collections of Roman and early Christian periods.

## 8. Veria (Berea)
### Key Sites & Activities
* **The Altar of Saint Paul (Apostle Paul's Tribune):** Site where Paul preached to the "noble Bereans" (Acts 17).
* **Old Metropolis of Veria:** 11th-century Byzantine basilica with surviving frescoes.
* **Byzantine Museum of Veria:** Housed in a restored mill with regional icons.
* **Church of the Resurrection of Christ (Agios Christos):** Contains early 14th-century masterworks.
* **Monastery of Panagia Soumela:** Houses the venerated, miraculous icon of the Virgin Mary brought from Pontus.
* **Church of Saint John the Theologian:** Atmospheric Byzantine chapel.
* **Church of Saint Blaise (Agios Vlasios):** Excellent example of "hidden" Byzantine churches.
* **Traditional Quarter of Kyriotissa:** Preserved Christian district with stone and wood mansions.
* **The Jewish Quarter (Barbouta):** Restored historic district featuring the old synagogue.
* **Archaeological Site of Aigai (Vergina):** Underground tomb of Philip II.

## 9. Kavala & Philippi
### Key Sites & Activities
* **Archaeological Site of Philippi:** Ruins of the first European city Paul evangelized (Acts 16).
* **The Prison of Saint Paul:** Traditional Roman crypt where Paul and Silas were held.
* **Saint Lydia's Sacred Baptistery:** Marks where Lydia, the first European Christian, was baptized.
* **Octagon of Philippi:** Ruins of an early Christian cathedral dedicated to Paul.
* **Ancient Theater of Philippi:** Remarkably preserved active theater.
* **Kavala Port (Ancient Neapolis):** Exact harbor where Paul, Timothy, Silas, and Luke first stepped on Europe.
* **Monument of Apostle Paul (Kavala):** Mosaic monument commemorating his arrival.
* **Holy Church of Saint Nicholas (Kavala):** Active Orthodox church originally built over an early Christian basilica.
* **Kavala Castle:** Hilltop fortress offering panoramic views.
* **The Kamares:** Arched 16th-century aqueduct through the city center.

## 10. Patmos (Daytrip)
### Key Sites & Activities
* **Holy Cave of the Apocalypse:** Sacred grotto where Saint John received the Revelation.
* **Holy Monastery of Saint John the Theologian:** Fortress-monastery containing relics and priceless Byzantine art.
* **The Aporthianos Path:** Ancient cobble path connecting the port to the Cave and Monastery.
* **Zoodochos Pigi Monastery:** 17th-century convent in Chora with beautiful frescoes.
* **Convent of the Annunciation (Evangelismos):** Known for its active nuns and rich spiritual life.
* **Church of Panagia Koumana:** Beautiful church in the bay area with rich local history.
* **Profitis Ilias Hermitage:** Highest point on the island, offering unparalleled views.
* **Wandering Chora:** Medieval, whitewashed labyrinth of alleys surrounding the main Monastery.
* **The Old Baptistery of St. John:** Traditionally believed to be where St. John baptized early converts.
* **Ruins of the Ancient Acropolis at Kastelli:** Remnants of ancient Patmos prior to its Christian history.
